- 04d-D4 [criterion 1, 2] 04d:74-76, 91-93, 405-408 -- The description of replication and locations does not match the generating model. AlphaSimR 2.1.0 (`calcPheno` body read): `reps` divides only `varE` (`addError`); GxE enters as `gxe_i * qnorm(p, sd = sqrt(envVar))` with ONE `p` per trait per `setPheno()` call, shared by all entries of the call. "Eight locations" at EYT is one environmental deviate, not eight; "a 'location' here is an independent draw rather than a place" (:407-408) is not what runs; "error variance has been divided by eight locations" (:75-76) is true of plot error only. Measured (300 `setPheno()` calls, 40 founders, chapter trait): accuracy cor(gv, pheno) with varGxE = 0.2: reps 1 = 0.40 (sd 0.14), reps 8 = 0.61 (sd 0.20); with varGxE ~ 0: 0.45 and 0.82 (sd 0.04). So the "entries versus precision" trade the chapter says it exposes (:23-27) is entries versus plot error under a per-call random GxE shift, accuracy at a stage-year swings widely, and the size of the GxE term against varG is never stated. Assumption used, mis-declared.
