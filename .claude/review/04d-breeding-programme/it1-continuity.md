Change 04d: do not equate the two; say proportional loss against own year-1 value. Nothing in 06 or 90 needs to change.

### 04d-C6 (H) -- owner: THIS chapter now; 04f owed (one sentence) -- "a location is an independent draw" (04d:74-76, 91-93, 405-408; 04f:2101-2103; 04c:383, 459-461)
04d:405-408 says locations differ "only through a variance component ... a 'location' here is an independent draw rather than a place". 04f:2101-2103 builds on it: "that chapter models locations as independent draws, whereas result 6 uses a genetic correlation between locations of Vg/(Vg+VgxE)" (2/3, R/17:54). In AlphaSimR 2.1.0 (checked in `calcPheno`): `reps` divides only the plot error `varE`; the GxE term is `gxe_i * qnorm(p)` with one `p` per call, the same for all entries of a `setPheno()` call. So, within one trial, replication does not average GxE, and "eight locations" (`rep_eyt = 8`) is one environmental deviate, not eight independent ones. The correct contrast with 04f is therefore not "independent draws (correlation 0) against 2/3": 04d samples no environments inside a trial at all and the stage-by-stage precision gained by `reps` is plot-error precision. 04d:74-76 "because the error variance has been divided by eight locations" is true only of `varE`. 04c:459-461 ("`reps` is the plant idiom for locations: ... error variance divided by `reps`") is correct for the error and loosely worded about "locations"; no change needed beyond that wording. Edit 04d (74-76, 405-408; state the size of the GxE term against varG, which is nowhere given) and then 04f:2101-2103 (owed to 04f, approved: the sentence only) together. The "analogy, not replication" in 04f stays true.
