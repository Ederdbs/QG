### Variance experiment, unlinked arm (11c:96-167)

- **11c-R8 [M] 11c:139-155 "t = -4.06 over 96 paired observations".** The 96 pairs are 8 seeds x 12 cycles, correlated within seed; the unit of replication is the seed. Seed-level: per-seed mean effect -0.13, -0.16, +0.002, -0.147, -0.106, -0.125, +0.056, -0.076; t = -3.16 on 7 df, 6 of 8 seeds negative. The finding itself is robust: rerun on 20 new seeds (101-120, `random` and `unconstrained` only, same producer code): pooled mean -0.113, seed-level t = -6.65, 19 of 20 seeds negative; early (cycles 1-4) -0.215 against late (9-12) -0.021, early minus late -0.194, seed-level t = -5.03. So "selection builds negative disequilibrium among unlinked loci" holds and "largest early, near zero late" holds in absolute units; the printed t and n_pairs are the wrong unit. The `constrained` arm is displayed but never tested (pooled t -3.82, seed-level -3.08, from the CSV); its cycle-3 value (-0.026) equals the random arm's, noise at 8 seeds.
- **11c-R9 [M] 11c:120-134, 165-167 the absolute gap decays because the genic variance is used up, not because the disequilibrium decays.** unconstrained `var_genic` (index units) 1.03 at cycle 0, 0.31 at 5, 0.02 at 12 (random arm 1.03, 0.78, 0.51). Relative to genic variance the selection effect does not decay: (var_g - var_genic)/var_genic, unconstrained minus random, by cycle 1-12 (8 seeds): -0.21 -0.26 -0.45 -0.32 -0.31 -0.36 -0.34 -0.25 -0.18 -0.28 -0.30 -0.46 (20 new seeds, cycles 1-11: -0.28 -0.36 -0.39 -0.40 -0.28 -0.36 -0.39 -0.46 -0.31 -0.43 -0.32; cycle 12 undefined, genic variance 0). var_g/var_genic stays 0.55-0.75 in the unconstrained arm from cycle 1 on (0.65-0.80 at cycle 5 in `cycles_trajectory.csv`, 0.64 / 0.78 / 0.94 for unconstrained / constrained / random in the 8-seed file). The chapter's reason "proportional to the genic variance still available" is true, but the table and prose present a decaying effect while the measured relative effect is a steady 30-45% reduction of usable variance. Mixed units (R-10) hide it. The chapter prints only cycles 0-6 (`head(w, 7)`), so the "by cycle 12" statement (11c:165-167) rests on rows not shown and no figure.
- **11c-R10 [H] 11c:158-160 "reaches a low equilibrium".** The measured equilibrium is not low: realised variance is 55-75% of genic variance in the selected arms (R-9), a 25-45% reduction every cycle. "Low" is typed, not computed. The sentence is the fix to 11b's C1 and replaces one unsupported claim by another. - **11c-R11 [M] 11c:114-115, 120-123 "two divergent pools add more".** False for a complete factorial. On the hybrid matrix `X` of all 25 x 25 hybrids the gap `var(X b) - genic_var(X, b)` equals the within-pool gaps `(gap_A + gap_B)/4` up to the n-1 factor (hybrid gap / ((gap_A + gap_B)/4) = 0.9615 = 24/25 in seeds 1-3 at cycle 0: -0.1210 against -0.1259; -0.1579 against -0.1642; -0.0978 against -0.1017). - **11c-R12 [M] 11c:124-125, 136-137 "identical in every respect", "Cycle 0 is identical across all three arms, to the digit".** Cycle 0 is identical (checked: max difference 0 in all 8 seeds, regimes random / unconstrained / constrained) but asserted by prose and eyeballed at 4 digits; no `stopifnot`. What I observed here (AlphaSimR 2.1.0, small config, not a controlled study; scripts `a11c/p.R`, `a11c/p2.R`): `runMacs2(nInd = 40, nChr = 5, segSites = 100, split = 100)` six calls after `set.seed(1)` in each of 3 fresh sessions, default threads: session A all six distinct and `.Random.seed` after the calls differing; session B call 1 distinct from calls 2-6, calls 2-6 identical, stream identical in all six; session C all six distinct, stream differing. With `nThreads = 1`: one session all distinct (stream differing), two sessions call 1 distinct and calls 2-6 identical (stream identical). Four calls of `alphasimr_founder_pools(rrs_config)` in 3 fresh sessions gave the same two patterns (first differs then identical in one session). The committed `limits_linked.csv` shows the "first call differs, later calls identical" pattern (R-14). So: `runMacs2()` founders are neither reproducible from `set.seed()` always (11c's claim holds in 4 of 6 sessions of mine) nor never; `nThreads = 1` did not remove it for `runMacs2()` in my probe; and the stream after the call was NOT identical whenever founders differed across calls in the all-distinct sessions (contradicting the "fixed amount" sentence there and agreeing in part with CLAUDE.md), while identical in the sessions where the later calls agree. Cause undetermined (a rejection loop over MaCS output would explain stream dependence on founders; not tested). What survives any reading and is what 11c needs: found once, branch both arms, `set.seed()` before downstream draws (done in `regenerate.R:622`). What does not survive: replicates sharing `cfg$seed` (R-14), "two calls ... different founder haplotypes" as an unconditional statement, and "downstream seeded normally" as an unconditional statement.
