## DISQUALIFYING FAILURES

- **11c-D16 [criterion 3] 11c:19-24 (summary), 11c:342-362 (section "Along the trajectory, not only at cycle 15", Result and Interpretation), 11c:301-302, 11c:436 and 11c:355-358; same claim index.qmd:131-134** -- the chapter replaces the claim "the forecast matches the trajectory" by two new claims about the same five-draw mean at alpha_max = 0.005: (a) the forecast "is low over the first cycles" (0.81-0.88 over cycles 2-5), so the 15-cycle agreement "is not a match of the trajectory"; (b) the series has "a first response below the following ones and a response that decays faster than Delta F". Neither carries any dispersion, in a chapter whose own previous section shows that the end-point ratio of the same means has per-draw range 0.58-1.52 and bootstrap 90 % interval 0.80-1.23. The unit of replication is the founder draw (n = 5) and the chapter has the bootstrap machinery in the chunk `rob-seed`; it was not applied to the new quantities. Per criterion 3 a qualitative claim on a mean with no dispersion, or a difference inside the Monte Carlo error, rejects. Recomputed with the chapter's own scheme (2000 resamples of the 5 draws with replacement, `set.seed(1)`, series averaged and refitted in each; `v3/b.R`):
  - ratio pred/obs at alpha 0.005, 5 % / 50 % / 95 %: cycle 2 0.78 / 0.87 / 0.98; cycle 3 0.65 / 0.81 / 0.99; cycle 4 0.64 / 0.84 / 1.05; cycle 5 0.69 / 0.88 / 1.09; cycle 15 0.80 / 1.01 / 1.23. The interval excludes 1 (narrowly) at cycles 2-3 and includes 1 at cycles 4-5 and 15, so "low over cycles 2 to 5" is resolved at two of the four cycles and the 13-19 % shortfall is not distinguishable from the end-point agreement it is contrasted with. Per draw (own per-draw fits) at cycles 2, 3, 4, 5: 4 of 5 draws below 1 in each; seed 5 above (1.04, 1.21, 1.31, 1.37).
  - R_1 minus R_2 (bootstrap 90 %): -0.40 to -0.02 (per draw 4 of 5 negative, paired t = 1.63 on 4 df for R_2 - R_1); R_1 minus R_3: -0.66 to +0.14 (interval includes 0).
  - R_15 minus R_1 (1 - dF)^14 at alpha 0.005: bootstrap 90 % -0.33 to +0.02; per draw -0.23 -0.03 +0.06 +0.04 -0.55 (3 of 5 below; mean -0.14, t = -1.23, 4 df, p = 0.29). So "a response that decays faster than Delta F" in the arm the headline is about (summary line 22-23; line 301-302 "all three arms"; line 358) is a difference of the five-draw mean that five draws do not resolve; in the other two arms the per-draw sign is 5 of 5 below geometric (own check), so there the statement holds.
  The author's side of the argument: the chapter says the section "rests on the five-draw mean and is in sample; the per-draw trajectories were not examined cycle by cycle", calls the offsetting "an inference from three numbers, not a decomposition" and writes "consistent with", and the numbers printed are exactly reproducible. That limit is declared and the interpretation is hedged. It does not cure the defect: the summary (11c:21-24) states as a result, without hedge, that the agreement "coincides with a low first-cycle response and a response that decays faster than Delta F, and is not a match of the trajectory"; the bootstrap that would show how far those can be said is already in the chapter; and the chapter treats the end-point agreement as unresolved (bootstrap 0.80-1.23) while treating a smaller shortfall at cycles 2-5 as a finding. The new section is the paired contrast "end point agrees / trajectory does not", and a contrast between two ratios each inside the other's interval is the thing criterion 3 asks not to be read as a difference. The same sentence in index.qmd:131-134 ("in sample and not along the trajectory") depends on it. What is not in dispute: the five-draw-mean values themselves, and that "the end-point agreement is a property of a mean of five draws" (already established by tbl-limits-spread).
