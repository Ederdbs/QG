## DISQUALIFYING FAILURES

- **11c-D14 [criterion 3, 4, 5] 11c:26-29 and 374-377 (summary and closing), index.qmd:131-133 (same claim), 11c:291-294** -- the headline says a Robertson-based forecast "matches the five-draw mean trajectory" of alpha_max = 0.005. The estimand actually compared (tbl-limits-rob) is one number, G_15, in sample. From the same fit (N_e from log(1 - F_drift), R_1 observed) the predicted cumulative curve against the observed five-draw mean at alpha 0.005 is, for t = 1..15, pred/obs 1.00, 0.87, 0.81, 0.84, 0.88, 0.92, 0.92, 0.94, 0.95, 0.95, 0.95, 0.96, 0.97, 0.99, 1.01 (observed G_t 0.80, 1.81, 2.89, 3.69 ... 9.98; predicted 0.80, 1.58, 2.34, 3.08 ... 10.05): the curve is 13 to 19 % low over cycles 2 to 5 and meets the observation only at the end. The chapter's own tbl-limits-rt shows why: R_1 = 0.80 is below R_2 = 1.01 and R_3 = 1.08 (my diff of the mean series), and the observed R_15 of 0.41 is below the geometric 0.55, i.e. the response of this arm also decays faster than Delta F; the agreement at 15 is an offset of a low R_1 against a too-fast decay. Line 291-294 says "it decays faster than Delta F for the two arms that overshoot", which leaves the reader to infer that the third arm decays at Delta F; its own row says otherwise. The end-point agreement is a measurement; "matches the trajectory" is not what was measured and the chapter's table and the cumulative curve contradict it. The same sentence is copied into index.qmd:131-133 ("matches the trajectory of the tightest budget in the five-draw mean") and, in softened form, 04d:1112-1114.

- **11c-D15 [criterion 3] 11c:493-497, 692-696 (and 508-511)** -- the central magnitude of the covariance section, "the realised variance is 35 %, 21 % and 6 % below the genic variance" at cycle 5, is a single-cycle mean over 8 seeds with no dispersion printed anywhere, and cycle 5 is not justified (why not 4 or 6?). Recomputed per seed at cycle 5 (var_g / var_genic): unconstrained 0.54 0.79 0.63 0.54 0.64 0.81 0.55 0.58 (mean 0.635, se 0.038); constrained 0.96 1.08 0.57 0.71 0.60 0.53 0.97 0.84 (mean 0.783, se 0.074); random 0.95 1.07 0.99 0.78 1.15 1.08 0.66 0.85 (mean 0.941, se 0.059). The "6 % below" of the random arm is one standard error from zero and the "21 %" of the constrained arm is within about two standard errors of it; the contrast of 21 % against 6 % is not resolved by 8 seeds and the chapter does not say so. Over cycles 1-8 the per-seed mean ratios are 0.685 (se 0.027), 0.787 (se 0.045), 0.997 (se 0.029): the random arm shows no loss at all on that window. The seed-level tests of tbl-limits-test carry the existence of the effect; these three percentages carry the size, are repeated in "The two costs of selection" as the result, and have no Monte Carlo error.
