## Limitations in the new text that the next review should check

- `tbl-limits-cum` is the five-draw mean only; per-draw per-cycle trajectories were not examined.
- The cycles 6-8 window and the early/late split are post hoc and declared so; the 20-seed replication covers the early/late contrast on the index scale, not the cycles 6-8 relative window.
- The cycle at which the slope's upper limit would bring the effect to zero (8.7) is a linear extrapolation from the window mean, an indicator of the width of the interval and not a forecast.
- `fx()` (fixed decimals) is a one-line helper added to the chapter setup chunk because `fmt()` drops trailing zeros ("0.8", "1").
