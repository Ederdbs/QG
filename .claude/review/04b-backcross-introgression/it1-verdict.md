DISQUALIFYING FAILURES

- 04b-D1 [criteria 2, 5; = 04b-C1, endorsed as disqualifying] qmd:164-178 against qmd:878-922 -- The chapter fixes $s = 0.60$ as the fraction of the genome donor and elite share *by descent* ($\text{IBD}_E = 1-(1-s)D$, eq. bc-ibd) and declares "Everything below uses $s = 0.60$" (qmd:178). The conversion-cost panel then takes an "unrelated line" as donor, sets the recurrent-parent-by-descent probability to `ibd <- 1 - expected_donor(3)` (qmd:895), i.e. $\text{IBD}_E = 1 - D$, which is eq. bc-ibd at $s = 0$, and says "donor-elite sharing is therefore already in the draw and is not added again through $s$" (qmd:880-882). State matching at 1/2 between two independent lines is the no-sharing baseline, not sharing by descent; the two readings of identity are conflated, and an assumption declared (s = 0.60) is silently violated in the section that carries the chapter's only measurement of the "near-copies" argument. Recomputed: expected off-diagonal ratio $2D(1-D) = 0.1172$ (D = 1/16) is the $s = 0$ value. Simulation with the donor shared by descent with probability s (200 000 markers, 8 conversions, 8 unrelated lines): $s = 0$ gives GD ratio 0.1170; $s = 0.6$ gives 0.0466, analytic $2D(1-D)(1-s) = 0.0469$. The chapter's own seed-11 draw prints 0.1250 (tolerance 0.15 asserted). So the headline 0.117 (qmd:915-920), the number that `index.qmd:118` and qmd:874-877 present as the measurement behind "near-copies", is not the chapter's $s$; at the chapter's $s$ it is about 2.5 times smaller. The direction of the conclusion is not reversed; the number and the declared assumption are inconsistent.

- 04b-D2 [criterion 1 and 5; = 04b-R1, endorsed, raised from Medium to disqualifying] qmd:235-236 -- "At most 47 plants per generation at four genes, and that is the whole foreground problem." 47 is the per-generation size for P = 0.95 (`n_for_count(1/16)` = 47, verified). The programme is three successive backcrosses and a lost generation ends the lineage. Recomputed: $(1-(15/16)^{47})^3 = 0.862$ (13.8% lost); the smallest N giving 0.95 over three generations is 64. The chapter's own scan shows it: `mabc_uniform.csv`, rec_drag, s = 0.6, k = 4, BC3: `p_lost` = 0.193 at N = 40 (300 reps), 0.050 at N = 60 (200 reps), and the chapter itself reports target loss at 20 and 40 plants per generation (qmd:544-550). "The whole foreground problem" is a quantitative claim about the programme that the per-generation formula does not compute; the opening of the "only one criterion binds" argument (qmd:211-278) rests on it.

- 04b-D3 [criterion 3, robustness] qmd:211-278, 380-388, 859-866 -- "Three criteria, and only one of them binds", "Recovering the elite is free by BC3", "the criterion that was already satisfied", and "the wall was a property of the index, not of maize" are stated without scope. The drag-priority index (rec_drag) was run at one value, $s = 0.60$ (audit: rec_bg alone at 0.5/0.6/0.7), one drag ceiling (30 cM per gene), one IBD target (0.90) and one 5 cM marker grid. The claim that background recovery does not bind depends on $s$ through the donor-specific fraction $(1-s)D$. Recomputed (rec_drag, k = 4, N = 200 per generation, three backcrosses, `make_shared_ibd(s = 0)` per replicate, 150 replicates, seed 100+N, one run): at $s = 0$ mean IBD at BC3 = 0.917 and P(IBD >= 0.90) = 0.713; at $s = 0.6$ in the same code, mean 0.958 and P = 1.000. At $s = 0$, the value the chapter uses in its last section, the IBD criterion binds. The sentence at qmd:770-771 ("at $s = 0$ none of it is [free]") concedes the cost but the section titles and conclusions stay unconditional, and "not of maize" is a statement about the generating model (Haldane, no interference, one lineage, s = 0.60) that the chapter's own limitations say is only a lower bound on required sizes.

- 04b-D4 [criterion 7; = 04b-B1, endorsed] qmd:805-807 -- "varies more than 1000-fold ... measured from breakpoints mapped at high resolution in nested association mapping populations [@rodgersmelnick2015]". The reference check read the full text (PMC4378432): the 1000-fold figure is background in the Significance and Abstract, not a result measured from its breakpoints (its own result: 136 000 breakpoints, hotspots threefold to over 100-fold). Attributes to the authors a measurement they did not make. I did not re-read the paper; I rely on the full-text finding of `it1-refs.md`.
