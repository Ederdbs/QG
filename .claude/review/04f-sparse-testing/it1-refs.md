### 04f-B6 [UNVERIFIED / possivel excesso] Hild Aono & Chawade 2026 -- :839-841 ("Below about 20 % the genomic kernel was preferable again")
FULL TEXT (PMC13365407): "At 30% data availability, performance varied depending on the specific environment combinations. Under the most extreme sparsity levels (10% and 20%), no consistent trend emerged; however, the [simbolo de kernel, imagem GIF ilegivel na extracao] kernel generally performed better in most scenarios." E: "Across all scenarios with at least 33% complete data per environment, the environmental kernel either matched or outperformed the genomic kernel." Portanto: "from about 30 to 33 %" e generoso (a 30 % varia por combinacao; o respaldo firme e >= 33 %), e "preferable again" abaixo de 20 % contradiz "no consistent trend" -- nao se pode confirmar qual kernel o simbolo representa.
