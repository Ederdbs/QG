### 02-R2 -- M -- F_ST attributed to Wright, while 08 calls the same expression G_ST (owed 00-C1)
`:222-225` ("These are Wright's fixation indices [@wright1951] written in coancestries; the form $F_{ST} = \bar D/(1-\bar f)$ is due to @cockerham1969, as restated by @toro2005"). 08 (`08-pipeline-stages.qmd:88-95`) reserves $F_{ST}^{(W)} = (\theta_w-\theta_B)/(1-\theta_B)$ for "Wright" and names $G_{ST} = (\theta_w-\theta_T)/(1-\theta_T)$, "equals Nei's $(H_T-H_S)/H_T$". Eq. `eq-f-stats` here is $(\tilde f-\bar f)/(1-\bar f)$ with $\bar f$ the coancestry of the pooled sample, i.e. $(H_T-H_S)/H_T$: algebraically 08's $G_{ST}$, not 08's $F_{ST}^{(W)}$.
Measured (30 random hybrids of `simulate_data(book_cfg)`, seed 7): `gd_partition()$F_ST` = 0.126279 = $(\theta_w-\theta_T)/(1-\theta_T)$ with $\theta_T=\bar f$=0.678547, $\theta_w$=0.71914; the 08 "Wright" form $(\theta_w-\theta_{AB})/(1-\theta_{AB})$ = 0.224241 (ratio 1.78). So 00:575-581 is right to say the two differ, and 02's name for its own expression is the one 08 contradicts. Which historical attribution is correct (Wright 1951 vs Nei 1973 vs Cockerham 1969) is UNVERIFIED: none of the three texts is in the repo.
Fix (02 side): say that $F_{ST}$ here equals $(H_T-H_S)/H_T$ (Nei's $G_{ST}$ for biallelic loci) and that the chapter uses it as $GD_{BS}/GD_T$; leave "Wright" only if 08 is made to agree. 08 is where the opposite label is wrong or at least non-standard.

### 02-R3 -- M -- `:676-679` states a result 05b does not show, in different units from `:354-359`
"under neutral drift every unit of $GD_{BS}$ gained is paid for out of the shared term". `@sec-heterosis-flat` (`05b:467-560`) shows that expected *heterosis* $\mathbb E[2\bar p(1-\bar p)]$ ("shared") loses exactly what $\mathbb E[y^2/2]$ ("divergence") gains at fixed $d_k\equiv1$, under Balding-Nichols draws; its terms are heterosis terms, not $GD$ terms, and "shared term" is not defined in 02. The chapter's own algebra at `:354-359` gives the $GD$ accounting: equal-weight closed pools, $\mathbb E f_{AB}$ unchanged, so $\bar f=(\tilde f+f_{AB})/2$ rises by $\Delta/2$ when $\tilde f$ rises by $\Delta$; $GD_{WS}$ falls $\Delta$, $GD_{BS}$ rises $\Delta/2$, $GD_T$ falls $\Delta/2$ -- two units of within-pool diversity lost per unit of $GD_{BS}$ gained, not unit for unit. I re-derived this (exact given $\mathbb E f_{AB}$ constant) and it is internally consistent; the clash is between `:676-679` and both `:354-359` and 05b's units.
Fix: state at `:676-679` what 05b shows (heterosis shared and divergence terms sum to a constant under neutral divergence) and what `:354-359` shows (GD_WS -Delta, GD_BS +Delta/2), without conflating them.

### 02-R5 -- L -- N_e statements: the checked chunk is an algebraic substitution; the "double" claim holds F_ST fixed without saying so
`ne-equal-seed` (`:559-568`) asserts `4N/(2+V_k) == 2N/(V_W+1)` at `V_W = V_k/2`. That is the substitution the text announces, so it cannot fail and is no oracle for the claims "4/3 for equal seeds only" and "2 for both parents". I ran a literal gene-dropping oracle (N = 100 monoecious diploids, 1500 unlinked biallelic loci at p = 0.5, 25 generations, 12 independent pedigrees per scheme, slope of log(H_t/H_0)): $N_e$ = 102.5 (random, $V_k$=2; theory 100), 132.8 (each plant mother once, father random, $V_k$=1; theory 4N/3 = 133.3), 200.2 (each plant mother once and father once; theory 2N = 200); $\Delta F$ s.e. 6.7e-5, 4.4e-5, 3.1e-5. Gains 1.296 and 1.953 against 4/3 and 2. So the single-population numbers are right; `eq-ne-meta`'s general form (`:548`, V_B, n subpopulations) and its section reference (@toro2005 §4a) are UNVERIFIED (source absent).
Scope gap: `:556-557` "(V_W = 0, V_B = 0) gives $2Nn/(1-F_{ST})$, **double**" compares two $N_e$ at the same $F_{ST}$; the asymptotic $F_{ST}$ of the metapopulation itself depends on the contribution variances, so the doubling is exact for $n=1$ (the case just verified) and "at fixed $F_{ST}$" otherwise.
