The mapping from the recommended metrics to table categories (alpha to "marker coancestry / kinship", N_e,par to "effective population size / status number", A_ret to "allelic richness / rare alleles") is never stated or argued, although each pair is a different quantity (pairwise kinship against a mean over a usage vector; effective number of parents against effective population size; the table's merged richness against A_ret, the very ambiguity D10 asked to declare). - 11-D17 [criteria 4, 7] 11:240-254 (last two sentences of the 298-line paragraph) and consequence 9, 11:641-645 -- After correctly stating the study's result (Wegary et al. 2019, abstract: nearly 91 % of pairs differ at 30-36 % of scored alleles, only 32 % have relative kinship < 0.050, read by the authors as one piece of evidence of "substantial redundancy ... due to repeated use of fewer genetic backgrounds"), the chapter says the two figures "show that a reading from state and a reading relative to the sample can disagree about redundancy", and consequence 9 says the study "shows state and relative kinship disagreeing".
