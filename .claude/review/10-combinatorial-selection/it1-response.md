## Findings of this rewrite that go against the chapter's older text or against 07 (recorded, not softened)

1. The DE (default `sel_de()`) is below the best feasible greedy at all five budgets on the 60-value grid, in 50 of 50 runs; it is above the 20-value benchmark-grid greedy at 4 of 5 budgets. The old "ahead at 4 of 5" was the coarse grid.
2. The DE **does** improve on a greedy plan when the best feasible greedy plan is among its seeds: +0.059 on average (range 0.028-0.075, 20 of 20 seeds), reaching 1.482 (bound 1.553, annealing 1.528 in 10b). `sel_de()`'s hard-coded seed grid (0, 0.25, ... 16) is 8 seeds of which 7 are the truncation plan and 1 is the w = 0 plan: two distinct plans.
3. `w = 0 only` is not distinguishable from `cold` at 20 seeds (+0.0075, s.e. 0.0097); `truncation only` recovers 47% of the default effect. The mechanism is not identified.
4. The pool-balance restriction cannot bind in an A x B factorial (every hybrid has one parent in each pool, so the share is exactly 1/2 for every plan; asserted in a chunk over 500 random plans). The cached column `pool_imbalance` = 0 in 9 of 9 rows is therefore structural, not an empirical slack. 07 reads it as "slack in every configuration" and "binds on two of four at most": still right in the count, wrong in the reason. (Inference about the cached run: it was a factorial, N = 3,600 = 60^2; the file does not record it.)
5. The DE's diversity constraint is soft in practice: 48 of 50 runs within budget; the two exceptions overshoot by 0.0003 and 0.0011 percentage points of alpha (about 0.003 and 0.011 index units at penalty 1000). The first exploratory 3-seed front (seeds 45-47) had all 15 inside; with 10 seeds it does not.
6. At the benchmark book scale `de_hybrid_cap` equals `tempering_cap` and `de_lines_cap` equals `ocs_round_cap` in index and alpha to four decimals (asserted); at production all five capped routes tie.
