## Findings

### 02b-R1 (M) :174-197, :186-196 -- the "disequilibrium term" of the seed-1 draw is sampling noise with expectation zero, and the chapter does not say so
Text (:176-178): "The difference is exactly the gametic-phase disequilibrium term [@bulmer1971], which @tbl-response-genic evaluates for the seed-1 draw". The table prints -2.7214 against a genic variance of 25.2051 (-10.8 percent). In `simulate_pools()` the 2000 loci are drawn independently, so over the complete factorial the hybrid covariance is exactly `Cov(x_k, x_l) = (Cov_A(u_k, u_l) + Cov_B(v_k, v_l))/4` (measured: max abs difference from the line-level covariances 1.5e-15; beta-weighted off-diagonal sum -2.717003 both ways). The term is therefore the chance LD of 25 + 25 founder lines. No between-pool term enters and no selection acts. Measured over 600 seeds: mean of the term -0.010, s.d. 4.57, negative in 50.5 percent; as a share of genic variance mean -0.0008, s.d. 0.20. Seed 1 (-2.72, share -0.108) is 0.6 s.d. from zero. A reader takes -10.8 percent as the size of the Bulmer effect; it is noise of either sign, and the identity is exact only as an algebraic statement. 11c:114-118 already says "the gap is not on its own a measure of the Bulmer effect", so 02b and 11c disagree in emphasis. Suggested: state the source of the term in this draw (finite founders, expectation zero here), give the factorial identity above as the exact reason no pool-divergence term appears, and keep Bulmer for what selection does (11c). Owed to 11c: 11c:114-115 "two divergent pools add more" is false for complete-factorial hybrids (the cross-pool term is absent by the identity above).

### 02b-R4 (M) :419, :428-429 -- the column "expected gain in moisture (trait units)" is the covariance of index and trait, not a gain
Caption (:419): "expected gain in the restricted trait, R' Sigma_G b, in trait units". `R' G b` = Cov(b'y, moisture) = 0.1430 (Smith-Hazel) and 0 (restricted); it depends on the arbitrary scale of `b`. The expected gain per round is `i R' G b / sqrt(b' P b)`: sigma_I = 1.1555, so 0.1237 i for Smith-Hazel (about 0.22 trait units at i = 1.774) and 0 for the restricted index. The value 0 is right (and the point of the section); the unit and the word "gain" are not, and 0.143 will be read as a gain. Suggested: rename "covariance of index and moisture" or multiply by `i / sigma_I` and say so.
