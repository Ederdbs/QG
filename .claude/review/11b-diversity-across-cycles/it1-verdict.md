## ON THE SUGGESTED RESEEDING IN R/14_cycles.R

Not required, and I do not make it a condition. The disqualifying defect is in the chapter text: it reads a trajectory difference between selectors as a selector effect. That is remedied in the text, either by dropping the trajectory contrast and reporting per-cycle DE minus greedy on the same context (what I ran above without touching R/), or by a paired design. Reseeding after the selector and before `line_weights()`/`next_pool()` is a behaviour change of an exported function: for the greedy and truncation selectors, which draw nothing, the stream would be unchanged (I did not verify that bit for bit), but the DE arm, `cycles_selector.csv` and the DE rows of the chapter would move, `devtools::test()` (test-cycles.R) and a full regeneration plus render would be owed. It is a legitimate way to obtain paired selectors and I would not object to it, but the author may choose either route; neither alone clears D4 (the evaluation budget, the 8-of-13 result, one seed and the dropped mechanism are text matters).
