## DISQUALIFYING FAILURES

- **11b-D15 [4, 5] `book/11b-diversity-across-cycles.qmd:336-341` -- the genic-variance inference is contradicted by the chapter's own logged columns.** The text says "the level of genic variance alone does not order the rates (the alpha_max = 0.02 pool at cycle 15 is far from the unconstrained one in rate and not in genic variance)". The numbers the chapter prints in the same paragraph are genic variance 0.001 / 0.08 / 0.22 and last-cycle gain 0.02 / 0.34 / 0.41 for unconstrained / 0.02 / 0.005: the two orderings are identical. "Not far in genic variance" compares 0.076 with 0.0009, a factor of 88 (0.28 against 0.03 in genic s.d.); it is "not far" only on a linear scale where both are within 7% of the founding 1.06. Over the whole trajectory the logged columns show the same thing: the per-cycle gain against the genic variance of the pool it was selected from (`var_genic` at t-1, seed mean, `cycles_trajectory.csv`) falls on one curve across the three arms: alpha 0.02 at var 0.102 gains 0.344, unconstrained at 0.161 gains 0.466 and at 0.064 gains 0.248 (interpolating about 0.35 at 0.102), alpha 0.005 at 0.257 gains 0.409, alpha 0.005 at 0.506 gains 0.660, unconstrained at 0.028 gains 0.137. The sentence that was written to downgrade "out of material" to a reading rests on a claim the data do not support; if anything the logged columns point the other way, and the chapter does not say so. The concluding hedge ("the simulation does not isolate the mechanism") is correct, but it does not license a false negative premise. This was an inference presented with evidence that fails on recomputation; it sits in the text changed since iteration 1 (the replacement for D9).

Every other iteration-1 disqualifying finding is closed. No other new disqualifying failure found.
