**11b-R4 (H) 11b:198-230 "It is not an artefact of the cheap selector" (owed 11b:201, 10b-C4/C6/C10).**
Cached DE arm = `de_samectx` rerun reproduces `cycles_selector.csv` DE index exactly for both budgets (`all.equal`, 13 cycles each, TRUE), so the following is on the chapter's own run. I added a wrapper that, at each cycle's context, also evaluates `sel_greedy_budget()` and truncation on the same `ctx` (seed 1, `NP = 300`, `itermax = 1000`, about 3e5 evaluations per cycle, 7.6-15.2 s per cycle):
- Unconstrained arm (alpha = Inf): DE index equals greedy index equals truncation in 13/13 cycles (difference exactly 0). DE finds nothing better there, as it cannot (truncation is the exact optimum).
- alpha 0.02 arm: DE index above the fine greedy sweep in 4/13 cycles, below in 8/13, equal in 1 (differences between -0.082 and +0.062). So "finding a better plan in every individual cycle" (11b:226) is false; 10/10b's finding (DE at this budget does not beat the fine greedy sweep) reproduces on this run. Only cycle 0 is a like-for-like comparison in the cached CSV, and there DE is +0.014 (0.020: 1.389 against 1.375) and 0.000 (unconstrained).
- The "unconstrained de" and "unconstrained greedy" arms select the same plan in each of their own contexts, yet differ by 0.60 at cycle 1 and 1.86 at cycle 12 (7.99 against 9.84). The only difference between them is the random stream: DE consumes random numbers between `set.seed(seed + 1000 + t)` (R/14_cycles.R:129, before the selector) and `next_pool()`, truncation does not. This is a direct measure of the single-run noise: the DE-versus-greedy margin at cycle 12 for the constrained minus unconstrained contrast (2.48 against 1.89, difference 0.59) is inside that noise (greedy margin over the five seeds: 1.89, 0.29, 0.91, 1.60, 2.12, mean 1.36, s.d. 0.75).
- "The crossover reproduces" (11b:218): in the DE arm the constrained programme is ahead from cycle 1 on (ahead string 0111111111111), against cycle 8 in the greedy arm; the early lead of the unconstrained programme, which is what the crossover is, does not appear in the DE arm, and the cause is the unconstrained DE path having drawn a worse recycling realisation, not the selector. The table (cycles 0, 4, 8, 12) hides cycle 1.
- "DE reaches a lower absolute index ... despite finding a better plan ... Per-cycle optimality is not multi-cycle optimality" (11b:224-230): the lower DE trajectory is explained by the RNG stream (unconstrained control, no budget to overspend) and by DE being worse than the fine greedy sweep in 8/13 contexts. The mechanism offered ("spends the difference out of the next cycle's inheritance") has no support in this run and cannot explain the unconstrained column. Remove it or test it (see fix).
- Typed number: "alpha = 0.0200 against greedy's 0.0177 at cycle 0": greedy 0.0177 matches; DE realised alpha is 0.0198 (0.0200 is the budget).
- The evaluation budget is not stated; 10b's qualifiers (30,000 evaluations: DE below fine greedy and local search) are not cited; the chapter cites @sec-combinatorial, not @sec-advanced.
Fix proposals: (i) in `R/14_cycles.R` call `set.seed(seed + 1000 + t)` again after the selector, before `line_weights()`/`next_pool()`. For the greedy and truncation selectors, which draw nothing, the stream is unchanged, so the cached greedy numbers should not move (not verified bit for bit); the DE arm would then share the recycling stream of the greedy arm and the unconstrained arms would coincide. (ii) Report per-cycle DE minus greedy on the same context (what `de_samectx.R` does), not the trajectory difference, and state `NP`, `itermax` and the evaluation count. (iii) Run more than one seed or say the DE check is one seed and a flag.

**11b-R6 (H) alpha is re-referenced every cycle; the chapter never says so.**

Also: under injection the realised alpha at cycle 8 is 0.0035 (closed 0.0158): the budget is slack there because the reference moved up with the pool; the chapter's injection section does not say the constraint stopped binding.
