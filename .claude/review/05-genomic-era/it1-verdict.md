## DISQUALIFYING FAILURES

- 05-D1 [3 replication, 4 result vs interpretation] 05:287-318, 05:1845 (checklist row) -- (= audit R1, endorsed HIGH.) The headline "truncation hybrids are more heterozygous than random subsets of the same size" ("real against the null", "beats the random-subset null on the very quantity ... product heterozygosity") rests on one founder draw (data seed 1, z = 2.51). I reran it over data seeds 1..20 (null B_full = 300, seed 7): z(F1_het, truncation) = 2.51, -0.41, 0.60, -0.66, -0.24, 0.58, -0.09, 0.59, 0.79, -0.85, -2.67, -4.47, 3.15, 0.06, 0.45, 0.45, 1.13, 3.33, 3.90, 1.38. Seven of 20 negative, 4 above +2, 2 below -2: no consistent sign. The unit of replication is the founder draw; the text reports one and builds the F_hom-versus-product argument ("F_hom would have called that plan the worst of the three for the very quantity on which it beats the null") and a mechanism ("truncation concentrated usage inside the pools ... the A x B pairs it kept are more heterozygous than random pairs") on it. The hedge "measured on one candidate set" limits the size of the effect, not its direction, and the direction is what is asserted. The robust part (max_div F1_het z between +5.8 and +8.7 on all 20 seeds; F_hom and F1 heterozygosity are different quantities) is not what is claimed for truncation.

- 05-D2 [2 assumptions, 3 selection bias/confounding, 4] 05:1299-1372 (Step 5, tbl-era-audit, tbl-era-audit-gaps, the Threshold paragraph) -- (= audit R2, endorsed HIGH; = part of C1.)
