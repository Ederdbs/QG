## Findings

**04e-R1 (H) 04e:223-225, 242, 943 -- "conservative for anything shrunk" / "conservative for sparse designs" is false in the chapter's own cached sweep.**
`gca_accuracy_theory()` against the cached 2,000-plot BLUP accuracy (`c`, realised mean `r`):

| design | closed form | cached BLUP | cached LS |
|---|---|---|---|
| tester k=2 | 0.660 | 0.657 | 0.653 |
| circulant c=2 (r=3) | 0.688 | 0.633 | 0.134 |
| circulant c=3 (r=3) | 0.758 | 0.727 | 0.255 |
| circulant c=5 (r=2) | 0.783 | 0.759 | 0.541 |
| random sparse c=5 | 0.783 | 0.740 | 0.689 |
| circulant c=10 (r=1) | 0.792 | 0.784 | 0.707 |
| NC II | 0.792 | 0.768 | 0.737 |

The formula is above BLUP in every sparse design (by 0.008 to 0.055) and 0.55 above LS at circulant c=2. It is exact only for a fixed tester set (partner GCA is a common constant), as V4 shows; for sparse designs the partners are unknown candidates and the formula ignores that noise. Consequences: (a) @tbl-mating-quantities rows 3-4 are written for "c crosses per line" with no tester restriction; (b) 04e:223-225 "a shrinkage estimator ... exceeds them" is true of the LS estimate in the same design, not of the formula; (c) 04e:943 "scores rho with the least-squares closed form, so it is conservative for sparse designs" -- the optimiser is optimistic for them, so the frontier ordering of c is not established. Fix: restrict the table rows and the V4 caption to tester designs, delete both "conservative" statements or replace by the numbers above.

**04e-R2 (H) 04e:439-441, 626, 855, 860 -- the SCA:GCA inflation figures are hand-typed, have no dispersion, and the statistic is not the ratio of the estimated components.**

The tester-versus-circulant contrast at 439-441 and the "Confirms and quantifies" verdict at 860 do not survive the change of statistic; the mean-of-ratios is the Jensen-biased one. Also omitted from the narrative: circulant c=5 (+15.6 / +12.1 %), 100 lines/pool c=10 random (+22 / +21 %) are as inflated as the testers, so "a tester design estimates SCA with the testers" is not what separates them. No replicate sd of the ratio is in the file. Fix: compute from the file inline, state which statistic, drop or soften 860 and the sentence at 442-443.

**04e-R8 (M) 04e:919-922, 960-961 -- cost narrative.**
"seed per cross caps replication, so a plan at one ear per cross has no seed for a second plot" is contradicted by `cross_cost_par()`: 250 seeds per ear / 60 per plot = 4.2 plots per ear, and the 2-ear minimum gives 8 (the chapter says so itself at 909-910 for two plots). No seed column is printed, so the claim is neither computed nor true at the placeholder levels. The "within 10 %" (9.7 %) includes 30,000 common cost (fixed + genotyping of 400 lines); the plan-specific part differs more.

**04e-R11 (M) 04e:178-185 -- the exact identity needs the candidate-set frequencies.** The text defines `delta u_ik = u_ik - p_Ak` with p "the pool frequencies"; the chunk's 2e-14 holds because `heterosis_gca_sca()` and the chunk use `colMeans(U)`, `colMeans(V)` (R/13_dominance.R:181-182).
